Withdraw cash

Moves cash out of the book, like a dividend: cash and NAV drop, P&L does not. You may withdraw more than the cash on hand, up to the NAV. Cash then goes negative and every buy is blocked until sells bring it back above zero.

Top up cash

Adds cash to the book, a capital injection: cash and NAV rise, P&L does not. Always allowed. Use it to lift a negative cash position and unblock buys at once.

Forget all cash withdrawals

P&L counts withdrawn cash as profit forever, which is why it grows so large. Forgetting writes off every withdrawal and top-up made so far: P&L becomes NAV minus starting cash. Cash, NAV and positions do not change. This cannot be undone.

Reset everything

Stops trading, drops and recreates every demo table, materialized view and view in Doris, and seeds a fresh book from strategy.toml: starting cash as configured (currently –), no positions, no history, nothing withdrawn. Takes about a minute. Type RESET to enable the button. This cannot be undone.
